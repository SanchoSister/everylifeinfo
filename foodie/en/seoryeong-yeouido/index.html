🍜Seoul Foodie: Seoryeong Yeouido — Pyeongyang Naengmyeon

A real-visit review (visited 2026.07.27) of Seoryeong's new Yeouido branch, tucked inside the 63 Building's basement food hall.

Address63-ro 50, B1 F05, Hanwha Life 63 Building, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul (서령 여의도점)
HoursDaily 10:30–22:00 (break time 15:00–17:00)
ParkingFree at 63 Building with a meal
MapOpen in Google Maps

About this place

  • Seoryeong is a well-known Pyeongyang-style naengmyeon (cold buckwheat noodle) restaurant, with its original location in Namdaemun and another branch in Jamsil. This Yeouido branch just opened inside the 63 Building's basement "Gourmet Street" food hall, alongside the newly opened Pompidou Centre Hanwha 63 Building.
  • The store is spacious and easy to spot thanks to its simple, clean signage. The interior has a wood-toned, tidy setting, with group seating available for larger parties.

Menu & prices

  • Seoryeong Sunmyeon (서령순면) — ₩17,000 — Korean beef (hanwoo) broth with pure buckwheat noodles
  • Perilla Oil Sunmyeon (들기름순면) — ₩17,000 — fresh-pressed perilla oil with pure buckwheat noodles
  • Bibim Sunmyeon (비빔순면, spicy mixed noodles) — ₩17,000
  • Plate Dumplings (접시만두) — full plate (6 pcs) ₩12,000 / half plate (3 pcs) ₩6,000
  • Pork Jowl Stir-fry (항정 제육) — ₩37,000
  • Dumpling soup (만둣국) is also on the menu.

The food

  • We ordered two Perilla Oil Sunmyeon, two Seoryeong Sunmyeon, and one plate of dumplings. The side dish of julienned radish (both plain and spicy-red versions) was excellent.
  • Seoryeong Sunmyeon: the broth was saltier with a stronger meaty aroma than expected — closer to a meat-broth style than the classic clean, delicate Pyeongyang naengmyeon. That actually makes it a friendlier entry point for naengmyeon beginners, and my teammates who ordered it liked the depth of flavor.
  • Perilla Oil Sunmyeon (my order): a distinctly nutty aroma from freshly pressed perilla oil soaked into the noodles — the first few bites were addictive. It got a bit heavy and slightly greasy toward the end, though topping it with the radish side helped balance it out. The savory buckwheat flavor still came through clearly.
  • Plate dumplings: the highlight of the meal — good skin thickness, generously filled with vegetables and pork, savory yet juicy. Six pieces per plate went fast even split between us; I'd recommend them just as much as the noodles.

Good points

  • Clean, spacious restaurant.
  • Uses the CatchTable real-time waitlist system, so you can browse nearby shops instead of standing in line.
  • The dumplings exceeded expectations.

Not so good

  • The perilla oil noodles get a bit greasy by the end — may not suit those who dislike rich flavors.
  • Seoryeong Sunmyeon leans toward a stronger meaty broth rather than the classic clear, delicate Pyeongyang style, which might not satisfy naengmyeon purists.
  • At about ₩17,000 per person, it's not the cheapest option for frequent visits.

Overall

  • Seoryeong Yeouido is worth a visit for the plate dumplings alone. The buckwheat noodles are solidly made, and with a clean interior and convenient parking, it's a good choice for a Yeouido office lunch or team dinner. It's better suited to naengmyeon beginners than purists — next time I'd like to try the pork jowl stir-fry.
